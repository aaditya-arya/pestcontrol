Ask our team
Common questions

Before your visit.

01How do I choose a treatment?+

Start with the pest you have noticed. If you cannot identify it, choose the unsure option on the enquiry form and describe the affected area.

02How should I prepare my property?+

Preparation depends on the treatment and the space. Discuss access, cleaning, food storage and re-entry instructions with our team before your visit.

03What is prophylactic treatment?+

It is preventive pest treatment for empty godowns, warehouses and storage spaces before fresh goods arrive. General pest control addresses pest concerns in spaces already in use.
