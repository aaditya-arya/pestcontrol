Clarifications on Fumigation & Compliance

Common queries answered regarding export standards, certificates, chemical safety, and response times.

ISPM-15 (International Standards for Phytosanitary Measures No. 15) is established by the International Plant Protection Convention (IPPC). It mandates that all non-manufactured wooden packaging (pallets, boxes, crates, dunnage) exceeding 6mm thickness must be treated to prevent the international transfer of pests and diseases. We treat packaging and apply the official recognized IPPC Wheat Mark stamp.

Once the mandatory gas exposure and safety aeration procedures are concluded, digital certificates are released same-day (typically within 24 hours of treatment completion) directly to your CHA, shipping line, or export documentation team with QR code verification.
