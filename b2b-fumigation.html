Before we get started.

Can treatment be arranged at our factory?+

Share your location and cargo details with the team. On-site availability and suitability will be confirmed before scheduling.

Which documents will I receive?+

The team will confirm the treatment records, marking, and documentation applicable to your shipment. For an existing document, visit our certificate enquiries page.

How long does treatment take?+

The treatment and aeration window depends on the cargo, method, and destination requirements. Please share your dispatch timeline when you enquire.
