Privacy

Your details.
Handled with care.

A straightforward explanation of how this website handles enquiries.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry forms let you prepare a message containing your name, contact details, location, and service requirements. Form entries are not saved by this website or submitted to a website database.

Sending an enquiry

After preparing a message, you choose whether to send it through WhatsApp or your email application. The relevant provider handles information according to its own terms and privacy policy. Details you send are used by our team to respond to and coordinate your enquiry.

External links

WhatsApp and Google Maps links take you to external services. Maps are not embedded on this site. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies in the website code.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser information, when serving website pages.

Questions about your information

To ask about information you have shared with our team, contact docs@ecomax.co.in or +91 84011 37910.
